Minimal Machines Publisher · last updated 5 October 2026

Minimal Machines Publisher ("the tool") is a command-line program that uploads videos to the Minimal Machines YouTube channel. It is written, operated, and used by Paul de Bruijn ("we") for that one channel.

Who may use the tool

The tool is not offered to the public. It is not distributed, sold, licensed, or made available to anyone other than its author. There is no sign-up, no account, and no way for a third party to run it against their own channel. These terms exist to describe how the tool is used, not to grant anyone else a right to use it.

What the tool does

On the author's instruction, the tool uploads a finished video file to the Minimal Machines channel through the YouTube Data API, sets its title, description, tags, category, thumbnail, privacy status, and scheduled publish time, adds it to a playlist on the same channel, and can post a comment on it. It does nothing on its own and nothing on anyone else's channel.
