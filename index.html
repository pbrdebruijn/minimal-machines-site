Minimal Machines

Small, quiet, slightly funny films about technology and the lives we live with it.

Minimal Machines is a YouTube channel of short animated comedy. A robot vacuum declares war on a cat. A smart fridge has anxiety at 3 a.m. A self-checkout machine refuses to believe a man about a lemon. Flat shapes, one amber accent, deadpan narration.

Watch: youtube.com/@MinimalMachines

Minimal Machines Publisher

Minimal Machines Publisher is the channel's own upload tool. It's a command-line program, run by the channel owner on their own computer, that uploads finished episodes to the Minimal Machines YouTube channel and sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail, schedule, and playlist.

  • Used by one person, the channel owner, for one channel.
  • Not offered to the public. There is nothing to install or sign up for.
  • Uses the YouTube Data API through Google sign-in. See the privacy policy for what that involves.
